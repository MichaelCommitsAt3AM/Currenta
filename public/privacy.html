Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Currenta. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website.

By using Currenta, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including:

  • Email address (via authentication providers like Supabase)
  • User profile information (name, avatar)
  • User-selected interests and preferences

2.2 Usage Data & History

To improve your experience, we collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This includes:

  • Article viewing history (stored to prevent redundant content delivery)
  • Articles saved to "Favorites"
  • Interaction data (clicks, time spent on categories)

2.3 Location Data

We use your IP address to detect your general location (Country and City). This allows us to provide localized news stories relevant to your region. We do not track your precise GPS coordinates without your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Data

Currenta uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service
  • To personalize your news feed based on interests and reading history
  • To generate AI-powered summaries of news articles
  • To detect and prevent technical issues
  • To provide you with localized content based on your region

4. Data Processing & Third Parties

We work with several third-party service providers to power our platform. These include:

  • Supabase: For user authentication and primary database storage.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini / Vertex AI): For news summarization and classification.
  • Firebase: For hosting our web components.
  • Upstash: For high-performance caching and temporary usage buffering.

These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

5. AI & Data Anonymization

When our AI processing systems summarize or categorize news, they do not have access to your personally identifiable information. We process news content at scale, and only use your preference tokens to decide which summaries to show you.

6. Your Data Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the data we hold about you
  • The right to request deletion of your account and associated data
  • The right to withdraw consent for location detection
  • The right to export your reading history and favorites

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@currenta.tech.

7. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, including encryption at rest and in transit via our partners at Supabase.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • By email: support@currenta.tech
  • By visiting our website: currenta.tech
