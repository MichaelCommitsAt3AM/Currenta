Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 25, 2026

1. Scope and Controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Currenta collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use the Currenta mobile app, website, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at support@currenta.tech.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Identity Data

  • Email address and login credentials (when you register/sign in).
  • Profile data provided by your identity provider (for example, name and avatar for social login).
  • Authentication identifiers and session tokens.

2.2 Preference and Personalization Data

  • Preferred country/region for localized news.
  • Selected interests and sub-interests.
  • Favorites, likes/dislikes, and article view history.

2.3 Location and Network Data

  • IP address and related proxy headers processed to infer your approximate country.
  • We use coarse location (country-level) for local content personalization.
  • We do not collect precise GPS coordinates through the Currenta app.

2.4 AI and Support Interaction Data

  • Article chat prompts and responses when you use AI chat features.
  • System context needed to generate responses (for example, article title/summary/source).

2.5 Device, Diagnostic, and Security Data

  • Crash and diagnostic data to identify and fix application failures.
  • Security and abuse-prevention logs (for example, OTP and API rate-limiting events).
  • Operational logs for ingestion and platform reliability.

2.6 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Cached articles and reading state.
  • Local chat history and chat sessions.
  • Local preferences and onboarding flags.
  • Session and auth persistence data, including secure storage where supported.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Provide, operate, and secure the Service.
  • Authenticate users (including anonymous guest sessions) and manage accounts.
  • Personalize feed ranking and local news content using your preferences and interactions.
  • Generate and deliver AI features (such as article summaries and chat assistance).
  • Send verification messages (for example, one-time passcodes).
  • Monitor reliability, investigate incidents, and prevent abuse/fraud.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on your location, we process data on one or more of the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract: to provide requested app features.
  • Legitimate interests: service improvement, security, and abuse prevention.
  • Consent: where required by applicable law.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required by law.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We share personal data with service providers only as needed to operate Currenta:

  • Supabase: authentication, database, and storage infrastructure.
  • Google Firebase: core app services and crash reporting.
  • Google AI Services (Gemini and/or Vertex AI): AI-assisted features such as chat and summarization.
  • Google Sign-In: social authentication (if you choose it).
  • Mailtrap: transactional email delivery (for example, OTP/verification emails).
  • Cloud/hosting providers: infrastructure, network, and security operations.

We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect rights/safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or asset sale.

6. AI Processing Notice

When you use AI features, your prompts and relevant article context are processed by our AI providers to generate responses. We implement controls to limit unnecessary personal data in prompts, but you should avoid submitting sensitive personal information in chat inputs unless necessary.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Currenta web properties may use browser storage technologies (including localStorage/sessionStorage) that are necessary for core site and admin session functionality. We do not currently use ad-tech trackers for behavioral advertising on the public site.

8. Data Retention

  • Account-related records are retained while your account is active and as needed for legal, security, and operational purposes.
  • Personalization and interaction data (for example, views/favorites/likes) are retained to provide your feed and history features unless deleted.
  • Operational and security logs are retained for a limited period according to business and security needs.
  • Device-local data persists until you clear app data, sign out/delete data, or uninstall the app.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including access controls, encrypted transport, and secure storage mechanisms where available. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own, including locations where our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You can request account deletion from within the app experience where available, or by contacting us at support@currenta.tech.

12. Children

Currenta is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital-consent age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child provided personal data, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the "Last Updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the Service or other appropriate channels.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • By email: support@currenta.tech
  • By visiting our website: currenta.tech
