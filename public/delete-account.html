Delete Account Data

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

1. How to delete your account

We provide two ways for you to delete your account and associated data:

1.1 In-App Deletion

You can delete your account directly within the Currenta mobile application. Navigate to Settings > Account Management and select the option to delete your account. This process is immediate and irreversible.

1.2 Email Request

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing us at support@currenta.tech with the following details:

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Required Info: Please send the email from the Google Account address used to sign in to the app.

2. What data is deleted?

When you delete your account, the following information is permanently removed from our active systems:

  • Profile Information: Your name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Authentication Identifiers: Your unique User ID associated with Google Sign-In.
  • User-Generated Content: Your saved articles (favorites), likes, and dislikes.
  • App Preferences: Your selected interests, sub-interests, and regional settings.
  • Reading History: Records of the articles you have recently viewed.
  • AI Usage Statistics: Data related to your use of AI features.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered. If you wish to use the app again, you will need to create a new account.

3. Retention Policy

Certain data may be retained for a limited period where required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., security logs, fraud prevention, or financial record-keeping). Any retained data will be anonymized to ensure it can no longer be associated with you personally. Residual copies of your data may remain in our encrypted backups for a short period until they are automatically overwritten.
